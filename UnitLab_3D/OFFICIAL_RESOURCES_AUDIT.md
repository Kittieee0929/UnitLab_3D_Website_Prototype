# UnitLab 3D — Official Learning Resource Audit

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-26  
**Scope:** Computer Systems Servicing / Computer Hardware Servicing — UnitLab 3D CBAR research prototype.

## Resource verification policy

1. Link only to resources published on official `deped.gov.ph`, `lrmds.deped.gov.ph`, or `tesda.gov.ph` domains.
2. **Direct PDF** indicates an official URL ending in `.pdf` with a document indexed by web search or exposed as a PDF by the document retriever. It **does not guarantee the government server will deliver the file to every browser at every moment**; some servers may block automated requests.
3. **Catalog record** indicates an official page with a description and possibly **View** or **Download** controls. The URL of the record is **not** the PDF URL. Where direct-file links could not be independently established, do not invent them.
4. **Collection** indicates an official index of documents with separate download links. Do not present its homepage as if it were one PDF.
5. The content remains hosted by the original government publisher. UnitLab does not mirror it or misrepresent it as UnitLab-authored material.
6. Distinguish the official curriculum and reference documents from UnitLab's independently authored instructional interventions and the research team's approved performance assessment.

## Source checks and limitations

- Original publisher records and indexed PDF descriptions were checked on 2026-10-26.
- Attempts to retrieve some official direct PDF files automatically returned HTTP 403 / cache restrictions; this cannot be interpreted as permission to host unofficial copies or to claim successful downloads.
- Prior catalog-only links in the website have been relabeled; a direct PDF link is provided when its original government URL could be independently established.
- Every lesson retains independent UnitLab prose. Official references are supporting materials, not an assertion that all UnitLab lesson text is a verbatim copy of DepEd/TESDA resources.
