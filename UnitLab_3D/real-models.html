ABOUT THE 3D ASSETS

Models come from the MIT-licensed open-source PC Anatomy project. Exact product names in our price catalog are labels, not guarantees that the geometry matches that SKU. Files load from its public GitHub repository.

If a model is temporarily unavailable, use the fallback viewer or continue with your drag-and-drop performance training. A stable internet connection is needed for this high-detail gallery.
