THE PROJECT

Developing and Evaluating UnitLab 3D

An Interactive Web-Based System Unit Simulation for Enhancing Learners’ Performance in Computer Hardware Servicing

Why UnitLab 3D?

When classes have only a few aging desktop units available, students may have limited chances to identify internal parts and rehearse safe disassembly and reassembly. This prototype offers an additional repeatable learning environment, not a replacement for physical hardware activities.
